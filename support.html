Support

Version 1.0 · Updated 28 September 2026

Email us: sori.korean.app@gmail.com

We reply as soon as we can, usually within a few days. To help us help you, tell us your phone model and system version, what you were doing, and what happened instead of what you expected. A screenshot is always welcome.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel Sori Pro

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sori, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sori, or play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  • In the app: Profile → Settings → Manage subscription opens the same page.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. After you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you already paid for.

Pro is not showing after I paid

Make sure your phone is signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account that made the purchase, then tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen. If it still does not unlock, email us with the purchase date and we will sort it out.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple or Google, and so do refunds: Report a problem (Apple) · Google Play refund policies. We cannot refund you directly, but write to us if you get stuck.

Playback

A clip will not play or says it is unavailable

Clips are YouTube videos, and their owners or YouTube can remove them or restrict them to certain countries at any time. Check whether the video plays in the YouTube app; if it does not, it is unavailable for everyone in your region and we will replace the clip in a future update. If the YouTube app plays it but Sori does not, tell us the clip's title.

Ads in the player

Videos play through YouTube's own player. YouTube may show ads before a clip, and sometimes in the middle of it or right after it. Occasionally the same ads play twice in a row; that is YouTube's ad scheduling, not Sori. Sori Pro does not remove them; it unlocks Sori's learning features. Watching is free for everyone.

The transcript is locked

On the free plan the transcript is available on the first 10 clips you open each day. The count resets at midnight, or Sori Pro removes the limit. Video keeps playing either way.

Korean audio

Listening exercises are silent, or show "install voice data"

Sori reads words and sentences with your phone's built-in Korean voice, and many phones ship without the Korean voice data installed. Until it is installed, Sori shows the reading version of the same question.

  • Android: Settings → General management (or System) → Language & input → Text-to-speech → the settings of your active engine → Install voice data → Korean. Install it into the engine the notice names; a different engine's voices are not used.
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Korean → download a voice.

Your data

Delete my data

Sori has no accounts and no server. Your saved words, progress and settings live only on your phone, and deleting the app deletes them. The two records that exist elsewhere, crash reports at Sentry and the subscription record at RevenueCat, are described in the privacy policy; email us to have them removed and we will tell you which detail we need to find them.

Report a transcript mistake

Transcripts are made with speech recognition and reviewed by hand, so mistakes happen. Email us the clip's title and the line, and what it should say.
