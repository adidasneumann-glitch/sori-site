2. What Sori is

Sori helps you learn Korean from short clips of Korean shows. Each clip comes with a synced transcript (Korean, romanization, word-by-word meanings and an English translation), you can save words as you watch, and review exercises bring them back.

The videos are YouTube videos. They stream from YouTube through YouTube's own embedded player, and YouTube's Terms of Service apply to playback. We do not host videos. A clip can become unavailable when its owner or YouTube removes or restricts it, and we may replace clips over time. Sori is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube, Google, or the broadcasters and channels whose videos appear in it.

3. Free and Pro

Video watching is unlimited for everyone, with the ads YouTube shows in its player. The free plan also includes the transcript on the first 10 clips each day, up to 20 saved words, and one review session a day.

Sori Pro removes those limits: unlimited transcripts and saved words, unlimited review sessions with spaced repetition, slow playback speeds and the sentence loop. We may change what each plan includes over time; a change that reduces Pro takes effect only from your next renewal.
